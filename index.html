AQUALANTIS, BEACHES & POOLS

Set beside a private white-sand beach on the Mediterranean coast in Belek, Antalya, Regnum Carya offers activities for guests of all ages. Aqualantis features a 2,400-square-metre wave pool, swimming pools, a water park, speed and tube slides, a lazy river, a rotating water slide and a children's pool.
